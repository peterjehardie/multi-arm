Help

A small DIY robot arm next to a rotating build plate. It swaps tools on its wrist: a hot end to print, a spindle to mill and a touch probe to measure. Everything happens through a physical thing: a wire, a gearbox, a heat path.

  1. Pick a job on the Jobs list and press Load.
  2. Press Run. Watch the job card and the scope.
  3. Click any part or wire in the 3D view to see what it is and what it is doing.
  4. Use Control to move the arm, change tools, open the door or run the fan yourself.
